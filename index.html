How It Works

The 10-step AI customer flow

Every job follows the same transparent path — from first message to next-service reminder.

1

Customer enters via Webpage / Telegram Intake

Webpage and Telegram act as the two main intake channels — customers choose whichever is easier.

2

Input vehicle info & request Intake

Car model, year, plate number, tire size, and the basic issue or service request — captured in one structured step.

3

AI recommends tires / service AI

The AI recommends suitable tires and a maintenance package, and suggests an initial inspection focus for the technician.

4

Book appointment Booking

The customer books a service appointment at a time that suits them — confirmed instantly.

5

Technician photos + checklist In-Store

In-store, the technician takes photos by phone and completes the digital inspection checklist.

6

AI vehicle report + 3 quotes AI

The AI compiles a vehicle condition report and generates three quotation options: Essential, Recommended, and Complete.

7

Customer chooses option Decision

The customer reviews the report and selects the preferred quote — no pressure, full transparency.

8

Service completed Workshop

Maintenance or repair is completed exactly as quoted.

9

System records mileage / parts / next service date Auto

Mileage, parts used, and the next service date are logged automatically to the customer's vehicle history.

10

AI follow-up & maintenance reminder Auto

The AI sends a follow-up message and future maintenance reminders — delivered through Telegram or webpage account notifications.
